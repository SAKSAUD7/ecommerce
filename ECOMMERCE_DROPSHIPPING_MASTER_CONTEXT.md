## 🛠️ Detailed Breakdown of Work Accomplished

### 1. Brand Identity & Global Contact
- **Monogram Logo**: Built SVG component `DeNouraLogo` with gold geometric floral emblem (`#C5A059`) and deep navy background (`#0A192F`).
- **Contact Details**: Integrated `Denoura.co@gmail.com`, `Denoura.co`, `Denoura.co.uk`, `@Denoura.co` (Instagram & TikTok) into footer, header, and contact pages.
- **Trust Badges**: Added 256-Bit SSL Encrypted Checkout, Free Global Express Shipping, 100% Authentic Modest Silk, and 30-Day Easy Returns badges.

### 2. Orders & Operations
- **Orders List**: Full order tracking, status badges, customer info, fulfillment states.
- **Returns & Refunds**: Created `ReturnRequest` model, API endpoints (`/api/orders/returns/`), stock restocking toggle, and admin approval portal.
- **Draft Orders**: Custom quote creator, wholesale draft invoicing, and payment link generator.
- **Abandoned Checkouts**: Recovery workflow with 1-click discount email triggers.

### 3. Products, Collections & Inventory
- **Catalog & Variants**: Full variant attributes (color, hex, size, material, SKU, stock).
- **Collections Builder**: Manual collections + Smart collections with auto price/category rules.
- **Inventory Stock**: Quick stock adjustments (+/-), threshold alerts (Low Stock, Out of Stock).
- **Transfers & Purchase Orders**: PO generation, receiving stock workflow, and location transfer audit log.

### 4. Dropshipping & Supplier Routing
- **Supplier Directory**: Supplier contact details, lead times, cost prices, order limits.
- **Auto Routing**: Orders automatically trigger supplier fulfillment sub-orders based on variant mapping.
- **Real Profit Engine**: Calculates Net Profit = Gross Revenue - Supplier Cost - Gateway Fees - Shipping.
